A note for single parents
Updated August 12, 2026

Raising kids alone after a car accident is a different kind of hard.

Nobody else drives the school run while your neck aches. Nobody else covers your shift, or answers the adjuster's calls, or opens the bills that arrive with only your name on them. When you are the whole team, an accident does not just hurt. It threatens the entire operation.

If that is where you are right now, this page was written for you, because there is one thing about your situation the insurance company understands very well. You should understand it too.

Adjusters are trained to settle fastest with the people who can least afford to wait. When one income runs the whole house, waiting feels impossible, and the first fast offer is priced on exactly that feeling. It is not priced on what your claim is worth. It is priced on how much pressure you are under.

The offer on the table was not calculated from your injuries. It was calculated from your urgency.
From one reviewed case

A single mother of two was offered $6,700 a few weeks after her crash. Once her claim was fully documented, with treatment, lost work, and future care counted, it ended near $61,000.
