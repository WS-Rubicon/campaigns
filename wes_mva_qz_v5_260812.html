Why the calendar matters more than people think

Insurers value files on what can be proven. Fresh claims come with reachable witnesses, clear records, and treatment that lines up with the crash date. As months pass, gaps appear, and every gap may become a reason to pay less.

That is why people who have the first number reviewed early tend to be in a stronger spot than people who wait. Not because of any deadline trick. Because the file itself is still clean.

In one reviewed case, a driver's first offer was $9,800. After the claim was fully documented while the evidence was still fresh, it ended near $84,000.
