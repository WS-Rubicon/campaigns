Quick Questions

How do I actually get the cash out?

Your equity is the difference between what your home is worth and what you still owe on it. A lender can advance you a portion of that as cash, secured against the home, and you keep living there. You can use the money for almost anything.

How much could I pull out?

It depends on your home's current value and how much you still owe. You'll see an estimate of what you may qualify for when you check, free and with no obligation.

What are the terms?

That depends on the option you're matched with and the lender. Amounts, rates and terms vary and are set after you apply. A lender will walk you through the specifics and what it would cost you before anything is agreed.

Does my credit matter?

Credit is one factor lenders consider, along with your home's equity and income. Homeowners with a range of credit situations may still qualify. Final rate and terms are subject to approval.

What can I use the money for?

Anything you want. Paying off high-interest debt, home improvements, tuition, medical bills, or an emergency fund. It's your cash to use.
